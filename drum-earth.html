📖
One Volume. One Journey.
The complete trilogy in a single volume. Daniel discovers the spirit worlds at university. Love, loss and a rescue mission in the Middle World follow. Then, across thirty-three years, Orlando and Dorian help him fulfil his ecological destiny.
🌍
20,000 Years of Spirit Cycles
From Lascaux caves in 20,000 BPT to Canterbury in 1420, Mexico City in 1521, to modern England and a 2070 epilogue — the story of DRUM EARTH spans millennia of reincarnation and consequence.

Unique Characters
Daniel, Francesca, Maisy, Stephen, Orlando, Dorian — each carrying the weight of past-life spirits. Animal guides: Panther, Bear, Horse, Eagle, Peregrine Falcon and Eagle Owl.

The Call to DRUM EARTH
At its heart, Drum Earth is a call to action — to save the planet from ecological disaster. The ORLANDOR Company's special trees and the 2070 epilogue make this a uniquely hopeful and visionary story.
★ Marketing Approach — Published Book
Unlike pre-launch marketing, every post in this calendar drives readers to buy, review, and share the book on Amazon and Goodreads right now. The strategy focuses on discoverability, social proof (reviews), and building a reader community around the trilogy. Amazon reviews are gold — this calendar is built around generating them.
The strategy

Three Months to Build
a Reader Community

The book is live. Now the goal is to find its readers, earn reviews, and build word-of-mouth. Month 1 announces and hooks. Month 2 goes deep into the story world and converts followers into buyers. Month 3 turns readers into a community that keeps selling the book for you.

Month 1 — Discover
Announce & Hook
Tell the world it's live. Introduce the three worlds, the timeline and the characters. Open the first review requests.
Month 2 — Depth
Go Deeper. Convert.
One post per book in the trilogy. The shamanic drum. Jet and Red. Francesca's twin. Emotional depth is what makes people finally click "buy".
Month 3 — Community
Sustain & Multiply
Book club kit, reader spotlights, author Q&A, the ORLANDOR mission and the 2070 ending. Long-tail sales come from conversation, not announcements.
All 12 Weeks
Always Amazon
Every single post carries a buy link and, from Month 1 Week 3 onward, a review ask. Reviews are the engine of discoverability.

Could the ORLANDOR Trees Be Real?
Quote / Insight
Facebook Instagram BookTok
Caption — Ready to Publish
The question I get asked most often about Drum Earth isn't about spirits. It's about trees. 🌳 In the trilogy, Daniel's children Orlando and Dorian build a company called ORLANDOR. They develop fast-growing trees with unusually large leaves — engineered variety by variety for the climate they'll live in, absorbing carbon dioxide at a far higher rate than a normal tree. Not one miracle species planted everywhere. Different trees for different places, mixed in with native planting so a forest comes back as a forest, not a plantation. They're grown, harvested for timber, and replanted. It's a working system, not a gesture. "But could that actually happen?" The specific tree is invented. Everything around it — fast-growing species, leaf area and carbon uptake, mixed replanting at the ragged edges of logged forest — is the direction real research already points in. I didn't want to write a novel that ends with the planet dying. There are enough of those, and they're all correct. I wanted to write one where someone spends fifty years on the problem and it works.
